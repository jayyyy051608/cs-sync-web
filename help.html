Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I sync my class schedule with my groupmates?

A: Head over to the Overlap tab to view mutual free windows and schedule coordination times with your project partners.

Q: Why is my UP Mail address locked on my profile page?

A: Official UP Mail (`@up.edu.ph`) addresses are bound to your student record for authentication security. You can add a personal backup address via your Profile settings if needed.

Q: How do I request a faculty consultation slot?

A: Check the Faculty directory to view available office hours and submit a direct consultation request.
